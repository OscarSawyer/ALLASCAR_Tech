Why Service Businesses Need WhatsApp on Their Website

WhatsApp makes it easier for customers to ask questions, request prices, book appointments and send details quickly.

Fewer steps means more inquiries.

Many customers will not fill long forms. A WhatsApp button with a preset message lowers friction and makes the conversation easier to start.

But WhatsApp must be managed.

Fast response matters. Use saved replies, business hours, greetings and clear handoff rules so inquiries do not die inside the chat.
