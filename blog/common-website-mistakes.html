Common Website Mistakes That Cost Businesses Customers

Many websites lose customers because they are slow, unclear, hard to contact and weak on trust.

Written by ALLASCAR_Tech

Practical website, Google visibility and lead generation guidance for service businesses.

The common mistakes

Weak headlines, hidden contact buttons, generic stock content, poor mobile spacing, no reviews, no service-area pages and no clear next step all reduce conversion.

Better approach

Start with the customer journey. What does the customer need to believe before calling? What information removes doubt? What action should they take next?
